Find the equilibrium index of an array (where sum of elements on the left is equal to sum on the right).

WTD: Examine the array to find an index where the sum of all elements to its left is equal to the sum of all elements to its right.

(e.g.: I/P: [-7, 1, 5, 2, -4, 3, 0], O/P: 3)


Approach	                                Time	Space	Reliable with negatives?
Recalculate left/right sums at every index	O(n²)	O(1)	✅
Your two-pointer approach	                O(n) potentially	O(1)	❌ Not generally reliable
Total sum + running left sum	            O(n)	O(1)	✅ Best
