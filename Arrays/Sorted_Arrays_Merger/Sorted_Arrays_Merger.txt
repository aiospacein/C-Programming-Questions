Merge two sorted arrays to produce one sorted array.

WTD: Sequentially compare the elements of two sorted arrays, combining them into a single array that remains sorted.

(e.g.: I/P: [1,3,5], [2,4,6], O/P: [1,2,3,4,5,6] )


Approach	Time	Extra Space	Verdict
Two-pointer merge	O(n + m)	O(n + m)	🥇 Best
Concatenate + sort	O((n+m) log(n+m))	Depends	❌ Wasteful
Nested comparison	O(n × m)	O(n+m)	❌
