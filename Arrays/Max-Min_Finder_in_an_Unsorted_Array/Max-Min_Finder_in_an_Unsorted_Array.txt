Find the largest and smallest number in an unsorted integer array.

WTD: Navigate through the elements of the unsorted array, continuously updating the largest and smallest values found to identify the extremities in the array.

(e.g.: I/P: [34, 15, 88, 2], O/P: Max: 88, Min: 2 )

Approach	                Time	Extra Space	Verdict
Single scan, track min/max	O(n)	O(1)	🥇 Best
Sort then take first/last	O(n log n)	Depends	❌ Unnecessary
Two separate scans	        O(2n) → O(n)	O(1)	Works, but unnecessary
Nested comparisons	        O(n²)	O(1)	❌ Avoid
